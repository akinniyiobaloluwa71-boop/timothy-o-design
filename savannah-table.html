Savannah Table

Restaurant Branding & Social Media Design

A complete restaurant brand concept featuring visual identity, menu presentation, marketing strategy and coordinated social media content. Savannah Table was created as a self-initiated project to design a full brand experience from the ground up — it is not a real restaurant or client project.

Category
Restaurant Branding & Social Media Design
Project Type

My Role — Brand & Visual Designer

I developed the visual direction and designed the restaurant brand concept, including the brand presentation, menu layout, promotional materials and coordinated social media designs.

Tools Used

  • Canva — primary design tool used to create the visual identity, layouts, menu and social media designs
  • Google Images — used to source suitable food imagery for the concept
  • ChatGPT — used for concept development, content direction and project planning
