About

A little about me

I'm Akinniyi Timothy Obaloluwa — the designer behind Timothy O Design. I work as a graphic designer and digital creative, helping individuals, businesses and organizations communicate their ideas through strong visual design and practical digital solutions.

My work spans logos and brand identity, websites, UI/UX, social media content and motion graphics. Whatever the project, my focus stays the same: understand what the client actually needs to say, and design something that says it clearly.

I pay attention to detail, I communicate clearly through every stage of a project, and I care about delivering work that's ready for real use — not just work that looks good in isolation.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I start a project?

Reach out via WhatsApp or email with a short description of what you need, and we'll take it from there.

What information do you need from me?

It depends on the project, but generally I'll need your goals for the project, any existing brand materials, and examples of styles you like.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity of the project. Once we've discussed the details, I'll give you a realistic timeframe before we begin.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Refining the design together is part of my process, as outlined in the "How I Work" section above.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes, I work with clients locally and internationally. Communication and file delivery are handled digitally.

How do I receive my final files?

Final files are delivered digitally, in the format that best suits their intended use — print-ready files, web-ready assets, or source files.

How can I contact you?

You can reach me directly via WhatsApp or email, or find all my platforms in the Connect section below.
